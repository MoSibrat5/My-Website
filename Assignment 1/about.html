About Bangladesh

Learn about the country and its cultural heritage.

Our Country

Bangladesh is located in South Asia and is surrounded by India on most sides, with Myanmar to the southeast and the Bay of Bengal to the south. Dhaka is the capital and one of the largest cities in the country.

Bangladesh has a diverse culture influenced by centuries of history. Bengali language and literature play an important role in national identity. The country is also known for traditional crafts, folk music, festivals and a wide variety of local cuisine.
